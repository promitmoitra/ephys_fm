Since report 001

Across days, calibration diversity is the lever. On two held-out cross-day datasets, a fingerprint trained on one day is fragile (BNCI2015_001 0.923, Zhou2016 0.807; a few people carry the errors). Trained on two days and tested on a third, it reaches 1.000 and 0.975. A data-matched control (half of each day) does almost as well, so it is day diversity, not data amount. The sealed phase gives each evaluation person several calibration sessions: fit the fingerprint on all of them pooled.

Pre-registration paid off. Finer filter banks won on BNCI2014 (0.994) but lost on both held-out datasets, so the 6-band bank stays. EEGNet + Riemann ensembles were below Riemann alone everywhere.

Channel count is not the story across days (BNCI: 9 channels 0.967 vs 22 channels 0.974), but it matters for many-way ID with little data (Dreyer 21 people from one run: 6 channels 0.869 → 27 channels 1.000). The sealed phase (10 people, 43 EEG channels) is comfortable.

Recommendation for Track 2

  • Replace the EEGNet fingerprint with the 6-band covariance fingerprint (research/src/torch_fp.py: pure torch, ~5.7 MB, matches sklearn to 4e-6), fitted on all calibration sessions pooled; keep soft routing.
  • Untested for the sealed data: whether its EOG/EMG channels should be excluded from the fingerprint (the mechanism says day-specific low-frequency content hurts across days; start with EEG channels only).
